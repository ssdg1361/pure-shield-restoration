**Last updated: July 11, 2026**

Pure Shield Restoration ("we," "us") provides mold remediation and water damage restoration services in South Florida. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect
When you contact us — by phone, through the form on this site, or through an ad or lead form on platforms like Facebook or Instagram — we collect the information you choose to share: typically your name, phone number, email, general location (such as your city or ZIP code), and a description of the problem you need help with.

Like most websites, we also use basic analytics (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site — pages visited, general location, and how you found us. This data is aggregated and doesn't identify you personally.

## How we use it
We use your information for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry and provide the service you asked about. That includes calling or messaging you back, scheduling an inspection, preparing an estimate, and documenting work performed (including for your insurance claim, at your direction).

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## Advertising
We may run ads on platforms such as Google, Facebook, and Instagram. If you submit a lead form on one of those platforms, that platform's own privacy policy also applies to the data it collects. Measurement tools (such as the Meta pixel and Google tags) may be used to understand whether our ads work; you can control ad personalization in your account settings on those platforms.

## Retention and your choices
We keep inquiry and job records as long as we need them for business, warranty, and legal purposes. If you'd like us to delete your contact information, or you have any question about this policy, call us at (954) 778-0925 or ask any time — we'll take care of it.
