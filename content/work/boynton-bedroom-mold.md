By the time we walked this **Boynton Beach** home, mold wasn't hiding — it had spread across bedroom walls, up into ceiling corners, and around the AC vents. That pattern usually means one thing in South Florida: a home that sat closed up with the humidity winning, so moisture settled into every room where air wasn't moving.

## How we handled it
The family packed up the affected rooms and we took it from there: containment on the worst areas, then room-by-room removal of the materials mold had rooted into and treatment of every affected surface, including the ceiling corners and vent surrounds where growth concentrates. We also addressed the moisture side — treatment without fixing the humidity just invites mold back.

## The result
Every affected room treated and verified, walls clean, and a home that's safe to live in again — not just painted over.
