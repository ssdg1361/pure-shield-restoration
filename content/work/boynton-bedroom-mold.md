By the time we walked this **Boynton Beach** home, the mold was out in the open, across bedroom walls, up into ceiling corners, and around the AC vents. In South Florida that pattern usually means the house sat closed up and the humidity got ahead of it, so moisture settled into every room where air wasn't moving.

## How we handled it
The family packed up the affected rooms and we took it from there. We set up containment on the worst areas, then went room by room removing the materials mold had rooted into and treating every affected surface, including the ceiling corners and vent surrounds where growth builds up. We also dealt with the humidity, because if you treat the mold and leave the humidity alone, the mold comes back.

## The result
Every affected room was treated and verified, the walls were clean, and the family had a home that's safe to live in again, with nothing painted over.
