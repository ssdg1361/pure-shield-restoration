Kitchens hide leaks well. In this **Deerfield Beach** home, water had been working its way along the cabinet run slowly enough that nothing dripped and nothing pooled. It just fed mold in the wall behind the lower cabinets, out of sight.

## How we handled it
We protected the floors and walkways, then opened the wall behind the counter to get at the actual growth instead of just what showed on the surface. The affected drywall and materials came out, the cavity was treated, and we extracted and dried as we went so the moisture that caused the problem left with it.

## The result
The mold removed at its source, inside the wall and not just off of it, and the kitchen dried, verified, and ready to close back up properly.
