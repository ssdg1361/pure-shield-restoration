Leaks are easy to miss in a kitchen. In this **Deerfield Beach** home, water had been seeping along the cabinet run so slowly that nothing dripped and nothing pooled. It fed mold in the wall behind the lower cabinets, out of sight.

## How we handled it
We protected the floors and walkways, then opened the wall behind the counter to get at the growth itself instead of what showed on the surface. We removed the affected drywall and materials, treated the cavity, and extracted and dried as we went so the moisture that caused the problem was gone too.

## The result
We removed the mold at its source inside the wall, and the kitchen was dried, verified, and ready to close back up properly.
