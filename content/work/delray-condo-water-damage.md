A leak from the upstairs condo caused significant water damage in this 55+ community condo in **Delray Beach**. Water from a failed garbage disposal and sink line traveled into the kitchen below and damaged the cabinets, countertops, backsplash areas, and wood flooring.

## How we restored it
We got there quickly and started the drying and cleanup. We set commercial **LGR dehumidifiers** and air movers to dry the affected areas and prevent further moisture damage, removed damaged materials as needed, and helped put everything back together, working directly with the insurance process the whole way.

## The result
We dried the kitchen to verified-dry, cleaned it, and restored it. There was no lingering moisture and no secondary mold, and the homeowner could stop worrying.
